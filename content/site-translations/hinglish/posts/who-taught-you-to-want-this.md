Kaafi lambe waqt tak mujhe lagta tha ki desire insaan ki sabse private cheez hoti hai. Kya chahte ho, kiske peeche bhagte ho, kya admire karte ho, kaisi zindagi meaningful lagti hai — yeh sab bahut personal lagta hai. Phir aap logon ko kaafi time tak closely dekhte ho — apne aap ko bhi — aur picture thodi ajeeb hone lagti hai. Itne saare log ek jaisi cheezein kyun chahte hain? Founders jo pehle ek acchi company banana chahte the, woh धीरे-धीरे valuation theater aur "kisne kaise raise kiya" mein kyun ulajh jaate hain? Satisfaction tab kyun girta hai jab koi nearby woh paa leta hai jo ab tumhe lagta hai tumhara hona chahiye tha — life worse nahi hui, phir bhi? Jo hum desire kehte hain, woh utni original nahi hai jitna hum sochte hain. Zyaadatar borrowed hoti hai.

## Desire social hoti hai

Yeh koi naya observation nahi hai. French thinker [René Girard](https://www.imitatio.org/) ne apni poori life's work isi par banayi — jis idea ko unhone mimetic desire kaha: ki hum cheezein apne aap nahi chahte, hum doosron ko copy karke chahna seekhte hain. Hum usually desire ke baare mein aise baat karte hain jaise woh andar se uthti ho. Apna passion dhundo. Jaano kya chahte ho. Shor band karo. Yeh advice individual ko flatter karti hai — yeh suggest karti hai ki confusion ke neeche ek pure personal signal hai jo wait kar raha hai. Real life utni clean nahi hoti. Hum seekhte hain ki kya desirable hai — doosron ko dekh ke ki woh kya desirable maante hain. Hum taste copy karte hain pehle — yeh jaane bina ki hum copy kar rahe hain — aur ambition bhi, yeh jaane bina ki humne use adopt kar liya hai. Iska matlab desire fake nahi ho jaati. Iska matlab hai yeh social hai — jo zyaada uncomfortable hai — kyunki iska matlab hai ki humaari kaafi strong ambitions pehle se doosron ke shape mein aayi hain. Kaam sirf yeh poochhna nahi ki kya chahte ho. Mushkil sawaal yeh hai ki kisne sikhaya tumhe chahna.

Zyaadatar log sochte hain ki desire celebrities se distort hoti hai. Hota hai yeh, lekin zyaada powerful distortions bahut kareeb ke logon se aati hain. Yahi hai Girard ka distinction — ek distant model jo fantasy banata hai, aur ek close model jo rivalry banata hai. Doosre desh ka billionaire ek spectacle hai. Tumhare batch ka founder jo tumse zyaada capital raise kar le, woh ek wound ban sakta hai. Ek dost jo woh ghar kharid le jo tum sochte the tumhara hona chahiye — woh restlessness create karta hai. [Luke Burgis](https://lukeburgis.com/books/), jinhone Girard ke idea ko business mein laaya, ise simple kar ke kehte hain: conflict hamari sameness se aati hai, differences se nahi. Ek peer precisely isliye dangerous hota hai kyunki uski zindagi tumhari zindagi se comparable hai. Hum sirf observe nahi karte ki hamare peers ke paas kya hai — hum unka significance ka scale inherit karte hain. Unke milestones hamare liye plausible ho jaate hain, unke status symbols naye tarike se visible ho jaate hain, unki pace ek pressure ban jaati hai. Isliye adulthood kaafi zyaada anxious feel ho sakta hai bachpan se, chahe material life improve ho gayi ho. Comparison ka daayra tighten hota hai.

## Rivalry desire ko cheez se alag kar deti hai

Yahan aake chahna dangerous ho jaata hai. Shuru mein cheez abhi bhi matter karti lagti hai — promotion, company, recognition. Par agar desire comparison ke through shape ho rahi hai, toh cheez dheere-dheere matter karna band kar deti hai — relative position zyaada important ho jaata hai. Sirf ek acchi company banana nahi chahte — ek aisi company banana chahte ho jo doosri company ko beat kare. Sirf wealth nahi chahte — woh wali chahte ho jo peers ke beech tumhari jagah rearrange kare. Yahi woh moment hai jab desire purpose ki jagah rivalry se feed hoti hai — aur ek baar aisa ho jaaye, satisfaction bahut mushkil ho jaati hai. Kyunki rivalry mein victory ka koi natural endpoint nahi hota. Comparison set update hota rehta hai. "Enough" ka standard doosre ki next move se continuously infect hota rehta hai. Log tab confused ho jaate hain ki achievement expected se flat kyun lagi. Aksar reason yeh nahi hota ki unhone bahut zyaada chaaha. Reason yeh hota hai ki unhone cheez ko khud chahna kaafi pehle band kar diya tha — aur shuru kar diya tha ki woh doosre ke relative kya represent karti hai.

Yeh problem purani hai. Scale naya hai. Social media ne doosron ki desires permanently visible kar di hain — ab tum sirf physically aaspaas ke chand logon ke beech nahi rehte, balki ek rolling aspiration theater ke andar rehte ho jahan koi hamesha better travel kar raha hai, faster build kar raha hai, zyaada publicly succeed kar raha hai. Chahe tumhe lagta ho tum immune ho — tumhara nervous system notes le raha hai. Aur yeh sirf consumer problem nahi hai. Yeh leadership aur company-building problem hai. Founders shuru karte hain kuch real solve karke — aur end up hote hain valuation envy mein phas ke. Teams shuru karti hain customers serve karke — aur end up karti hain us company ke prestige rituals copy karke jo is waqt fashionable hai. Hum sochte hain ki choice ki freedom se self-authored lives banti hain. Aksar ulta hota hai — unconscious imitation ke zyaada mauke milte hain.

Isliye yeh topic mere liye matter karta hai. Khatra sirf yeh nahi ki log shallow cheezein chase karte hain. Zyaada interesting khatra yeh hai ki borrowed desire seriousness ka disguise pehen sakti hai. Woh discipline ki tarah dikh sakti hai, yahan tak ki courage ki tarah bhi — kyunki kabhi kabhi jo hum courage kehte hain woh sirf ek borrowed script ke liye suffer karne ki willingness hai. Isliye simple anti-ambition language kaam nahi aati. Jawab yeh nahi ki kuch mat chahho. Imitation human hone ka hissa hai — hum seekhte hain, build karte hain, bante hain models ke zariye. Asli sawaal yeh hai ki kya hum unhe consciously choose kar rahe hain. Kuch models tumhe zyaada grounded aur zyaada free chodh jaate hain. Doosre tumhe zyaada agitated aur performance ke andar zyaada trapped chodh jaate hain. Dono bahar se impressive dikh sakte hain. Sirf ek tumhe apne aap se kam divided chodha jaata hai.

## Saaf ambition

Loop ko force se nahi todte. Compare karna shayad sheer will se band nahi hota. Woh band hota hai jab tum un conditions ko change karte ho jisme desire form hoti hai. Tum change karte ho ki kin logon ki zindagi tum bahut closely study karte ho. Tum un ambitions par zyaada suspicious hone lagte ho jo tab intensify hoti hain jab koi peer succeed karta hai — aur tab fade ho jaati hain jab koi dekh nahi raha. Aur tum better sawaal poochhhte ho. Agar yeh achieve kar liya aur kisi ko pata na chala — kya tab bhi chahiye yeh? Agar woh insaan jisse main secretly compare karta hoon — woh hata diya jaaye, kya yeh goal abhi bhi alive lagega? Kya yeh admiration hai, ya rivalry jo acche kapde pehne hue hai? Zyaadatar logon ko kam ambition nahi chahiye. Unhe saaf ambition chahiye.

Mujhe nahi lagta goal yeh hai ki desire se detached ho jaao. Desire hi zindagi ko animate karti hai. Woh craft, invention, devotion create karti hai. Problem chahna nahi hai. Problem yeh hai ki hum sleepwalk karte hain un wants mein jo socially install ki gayi hain par personally kabhi examine nahi ki gayi. Hum ek doosre ko hamesha sikhate rehte hain ki kya matter karta hai — parents, founders, friends, poori companies — sirf effort model nahi karte balki aspiration bhi. Isliye log saalon tak kisi cheez ki taraf climb karte hain aur pahunchne par strangely absent feel karte hain.

Main bhi exempt nahi hoon. Maine numbers isliye chahhe hain kyunki kisi doosre founder ne pehle announce kiye. Aur sabse useful feedback jo maine kabhi li hai woh isi essay ka ek version tha jo mujhe hi wapas diya gaya: doosre founders ko itna closely mat study karo, apna khud ka style define karo, aur uspar itna believe karo ki woh tumhara apna ban jaaye. Toh main ek sawaal par baar baar aata hoon jo ambition ke baare mein almost kisi bhi advice se zyaada useful ho gaya hai mere liye. Kya chahta hoon — nahi. Kisne sikhaya mujhe yeh chahna.

Yeh sawaal desire ko khatam nahi karta. Yeh use saaf karta hai.
