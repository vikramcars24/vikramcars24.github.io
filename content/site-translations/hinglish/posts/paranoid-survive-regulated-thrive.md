[Cars24](https://www.cars24.com) build karte karte ek decade se zyaada ho gaya hai — aur jo worst decisions maine liye, woh zyaadatar gusse mein liye. Ya anxiety mein. Ya jab kisi baat ne sting kiya. Baad mein samjha, jab thanda pada aur call already ho chuki thi. Yahi woh cheez hai jo koi nahi batata jab tum company build kar rahe ho. Jo pressure tumhe drive karta hai, wahi pressure tumhara judgment barbad bhi karta hai.

Andy Grove ne humein woh line di jis par hum sab jeete hain: only the paranoid survive. Maine isi par jeya hai. Hungry. Urgent. Bar raise karo, aur jis din clear karo, fir raise karo.

Lekin Grove ko dhyan se padho. Uski paranoia kabhi panic nahi thi. Usne use aim kiya. Strategic inflection points par — woh moments jab ek business completely remake hone wala hota hai. Specific fears par jo actually worth having thi. Yeh ek stance thi jo usne adopt ki thi, koi permanent state nahi jisme woh rehta tha. Zyaadatar leaders yeh distinction miss kar dete hain. Woh vigilance ke baare mein ek sharp instruction ko permanent dread ki condition mein badal dete hain. Phir sochte hain ki best people burn out kyun kar rahe hain aur judgment kyun ja raha hai.

Survival kabhi point nahi tha. Thriving hai. Aur [Naina Sahni](https://workwithnaina.com) — jo pichhle do saalon se meri coach hain — unhone mujhe dikhaya ki Grove ka law sirf adha sentence hai. The paranoid survive. The regulated thrive. Doosra adha unka hai. Yeh essay mera us baat ko jeena seekhna hai.

Yeh dono opposite lagte hain. Relentlessly push karo, zyaada maango, kabhi satisfied mat ho. Aur saath mein — calm raho, kind raho, apne saath waale logon ka dhyan rakho. Sabse sharp example jo mujhe pata hai woh koi company nahi hai. Ek rugby team hai. New Zealand ki All Blacks ne ek sadi se zyaada samay mein apne khele gaye 75 percent se zyaada matches jeete hain — kisi bhi sport mein kisi bhi team ka yeh ek aisa record hai jo bahut kam hi match karta hai. Woh standards ke baare mein merciless hain. Aur woh ek tool bhi use karte hain jise red head, blue head kehte hain — yeh unki drilling mein hai. Red head tight hai, panicked, overwhelmed. Blue head calm hai, clear, accurate. Sabse heavy pressure mein, woh practice karte hain ek se doosre par shift karne ki. Duniya ki sabse demanding team hot run karke nahi jeetti. Woh apne logon ko command par thanda hona sikhaa kar jeetti hai.

Body explain karti hai kyun. Yale ki Amy Arnsten ne dikhaya hai ki mild, uncontrollable stress bhi exactly un faculties ka rapid loss cause karta hai jiske liye ek leader ko pay kiya jaata hai. Judgment. Working memory. Plan karne ki ability. Chronic pressure mein thinking brain quiet ho jaata hai aur reflexive, fearful brain wheel pakad leta hai. Athletes yeh curve achi tarah jaante hain. Jo sabse zyaada rest ke bina train karta hai woh peak nahi karta. Wo toot jaata hai. Effort badhta raha, performance girta raha. Permanently hot chalti company bhi yahi karti hai.

Toh regulation ambition ka soft sibling nahi hai. Yeh woh cheez hai jo ambition ko kaam karte rehne deti hai. Aur yeh ek discipline se shuru hoti hai jo maine hard way se seekhi. Emotion feel karo. Decision uske andar mat lo.

Hum insaan hain. Buri news aati hai, koi disagree karta hai, plan slip karta hai — aur hum feel karte hain. Yeh koi flaw nahi hai jise engineer out kiya jaaye. Flaw yeh hai ki feeling aur decision ko ek hi moment mein collapse kar do. Daniel Goleman ne ise amygdala hijack kaha. Brain ka alarm controls seizure karta hai ek beat pehle, thinking mind ke vote karne se pehle. Kahneman ne yahi warning ko alag naam diya — ek fast system ek slow system ko outrun karta hua. Leadership ka kaam un dono ke beech ka gap badhana hai.

Lekin iska matlab numb ho jaana mat samjho. Antonio Damasio ne unhe study kiya jinke brain ka emotional hissa chala gaya tha, reasoning intact thi. Woh better decide nahi karte the. Woh worse decide karte the. Bahut se decide hi nahi kar paate the. Emotion information hai. Skill, jaisi James Gross ki research dikhati hai, yeh nahi hai ki ise bottle up kar lo — jo sirf tumhe drain karta hai jabki feeling bani rehti hai. Skill yeh hai ki feel karo, reframe karo, aur act karo jab woh guzar jaaye.

Kuch log regulated ko slow ka softer word samjhenge. Yeh bilkul ulta hai. Dangerous calm woh composed chehra hai jo buri news ko daba deta hai aur use maturity kehta hai. Woh regulation nahi hai. Woh suppression hai, aur yeh judgment aur company dono ko worse banata hai. Regulated ka matlab yeh nahi ki tum kam feel karte ho. Matlab yeh hai ki sab kuch feel karte ho aur phir bhi decision ko apne us hisse ko nahi dete jo panic kar raha hai. Frantic founder aur falsely serene founder — dono opposite ends se wahi galti karte hain. Dono feeling ko drive karne dete hain.

Ambitious leaders ek cheez ulti samjhte hain. Compassion ko ek reward ki tarah treat karte hain jo performance ke baad dete hain. Woh asliyat mein woh cheez hai jo performance ko possible banati hai. Google ne study kiya ki uski best teams ko best kya banata hai. Top factor talent nahi tha. Hours nahi. Psychological safety thi. Risk lene ya galti maanne ki confidence, bina humiliate hue. Amy Edmondson, jinhone yeh idea name kiya, seedha kehti hain ki yeh nice hone ke baare mein nahi hai. High care aur high standards — saath mein. Kim Scott isse chaar shabdon mein kehti hain. Care personally, challenge directly. Ek dara hua team exactly unhi threats ko chhupa leta hai jo ek paranoid leader ko sabse pehle dekhne chahiye. Kyunki dar — yahi science humein batati hai — wahi cheez hai jo thinking brain ko offline le jaata hai. Compassion woh nahi hai jo humein slow karta hai. Yahi cheez hai jo ek high standard ko real logon ke saath contact mein survive karne deti hai.

Naina mechanism ko mere se zyaada precisely name karti hain. Log ek darte hue leader se jhooth nahi bolte. Woh calibrate karte hain. Sach ko soften karte hain, time karte hain, sideways route karte hain. Knowledge company mein exist karti rehti hai. Bas woh us insaan tak pohonchna band ho jaati hai jise us par act karne ke liye pay kiya jaata hai. Aur leader ko yeh loss bilkul nahi lagta. Kam buri surprises ek well-run company lagti hai. Yahi symptom hai.

Aur yeh sab train kiya ja sakta hai. Yeh part mujhe sabse seriously lagta hai. Calm koi temperament nahi hai jo birth se milta ho. Yeh ek capacity hai jo tum build karte ho, aur body mein build karte ho. Heart rate variability ek real — agar imperfect — signal hai ki tum andar se kitne regulated ho. Yeh practice se respond karta hai. Isliye mujhe ek physical trainer aur mental coaches paas mein chahiye, aur main unhe use karta hoon. Mera kaam ab sirf meetings aur reviews ke woh ghante nahi hain. Body aur mind par woh ghante bhi kaam hain. Wahi gym hai jahan calm under fire build hoti hai.

Yeh private bhi nahi rehta. Navy SEALs ne ise teen shabdon mein rakha hai. Calm is contagious. Panic bhi. Chaos bhi. Team tumhari strategy catch nahi karti. Woh tumhara nervous system catch karti hai, aur use amplify karti hai. Ek dysregulated founder ek dysregulated company build karta hai. Ek calm wala ek calm company. Jinhe main lead karta hoon unke liye sabse highest-leverage cheez jo main kar sakta hoon woh harder push karna nahi hai. Woh room mein sabse steady insaan hona hai jab news sabse buri ho.

Toh main do cheezein banna chahta hoon jinhe duniya opposite kehti hai. Kaam ke baare mein paranoid. Khud mein regulated. Frantic hue bina hungry. Cruel hue bina demanding. Yeh combination rare hai. Aur exactly isliye jeetta hai.

The paranoid survive. The regulated thrive. Main dono ke liye build kar raha hoon.
