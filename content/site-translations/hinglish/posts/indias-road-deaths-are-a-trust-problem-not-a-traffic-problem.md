India mein mushkil se koi aisa ghar hoga jiske paas apna koi crash story na ho. Ek baap jo kabhi ghar nahi aaya. Ek cousin jiska pair kabhi theek nahi hua. Ek bachcha jo school gate ke bahar accident mein aa gaya. Ek delivery rider jiska naam news cycle se gayab ho gaya, bahut pehle, jab uski family abhi uth bhi nahi payi thi.

Meri khud ki story 2005 ki hai. Main IIT Bombay mein tha. Ek raat main aur mera ek batchmate Mumbai mein late night Kinetic par wapas aa rahe the. Wo drive kar raha tha, main pillion par. Hum ek divider waqt par dekh nahi sake. Scooter ussey laga aur woh sadak ke paar ja gira. Main is taraf gira. Ek taxi ne use ronda. Hum ne use uthaya aur poore raaste hospital tak main use godi mein liye baitha raha. Woh nahi bacha. Main bina ek khraonch ke nikal aaya.

Jo cheez mere saath rahi woh sirf dard nahi thi. Woh be-insafi thi. Same gaadi, same raat, same crash. Ek bacha, ek nahi.

Yahi woh ajeeb jagah hai jahan sadak ki maut Indian life mein rehti hai. Itni personal ki har ghar ko chhoo jaaye, itni aam ki normal lagti ho.

2023 mein [Ministry of Road Transport and Highways](https://morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/Road-Accident-in-India-2023-Publications.pdf) ne 1,72,890 sadak mautein darj ki — India ka ab tak ka sabse uncha figure. Yeh bhi almost certainly ek undercount hai. [WHO](https://www.who.int/india/health-topics/road-safety) ne kabhi estimate kiya tha ki 2016 mein India ka asli toll lagbhag 3,00,000 tha — us saal ke official figure se kaafi zyada — aur government ka apna Sample Registration System bhi 2022 ki mautein police count se kaafi upar rakhta hai. Exact number zaroori hai. Lekin isse bhi zyada zaroori yeh hai ki hum abhi bhi ek number par agree nahi kar paate. Jo desh apni sadak mautein theek se count nahi kar sakta, woh sirf traffic se nahi, sach se jujh raha hai.

Baat ka pivot hamesha visible layer par rehta hai: overspeeding, drunk driving, kharab sadken, helmets nahi, enforcement kamzor. Sab sach hai. Lekin koi bhi sabse gehri failure tak nahi pahunchta — yeh ki crash ke aas-paas ka system itna trustworthy nahi hai ki kaam kar sake.

Main trust ko optimism ya civic niceness ki tarah nahi bol raha. Main ise operationally bol raha hoon. Kya institutions us data par trust kar sakti hain jo woh use kar rahi hain? Kya ek bystander trust kar sakta hai ki victim ki madad karna use saza dilayega? Kya ek family trust kar sakti hai ki compensation asli hai aur unki pahunch mein hai? Kya engineer, police officer, hospital, aur judge sab ek hi picture dekh rahe hain — kya hua tha? Aksar jawab na hota hai.

Yeh koi fringe reading nahi hai. [Piyush Tewari ne SaveLIFE Foundation shuru ki](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/a_new_model_for_saving_lives_on_roads_around_the_world) ek teenage cousin ko crash mein kho dene ke baad, aur Mumbai-Pune Expressway ka ek hissa Safe System approach par rebuild kiya — yeh premise ki log galti karenge, chahe kitni bhi training ho, aur unke aas-paas ka system us galti ko absorb karne ke liye hona chahiye. 3,500 se zyada engineering defects theek kar ke aur ambulance response time ko karib 35 minute se 10 minute ke andar la kar, us single corridor par 2016 aur 2020 ke beech mautein karib aadhi ho gayi. Lesson yeh nahi ki wahan ke drivers achanak better ho gaye. Lesson yeh hai ki system ne unki galtiyon ko fatal hone se rokna band kar diya.

Main yahan kisi safe distance se nahi pahuncha. Na hi Amar Srivastava aur Deepanshu Gupta, jinhone Indian Road Safety Campaign shuru ki aur [Crashfree India](https://www.crashfreeindia.org/) banaya [Gajendra Jangid](https://www.cars24.com/about-us) ke saath, jo [Cars24](https://www.cars24.com) mein mere co-founder hain. Pichle aath-das mahine ki us kaam ne failure ko kitne kareeb se dikhaya — kisi bhi opinion se zyada — aur ek hi pattern baar baar saamne aata raha: breakdowns aapas mein connected hain.

**Ek system jo dekh nahi sakta**

Shuru karte hain data se. India mein road death ke kai systems hain jo reconcile nahi karte: police-based MoRTH aur NCRB figures, survey-based Sample Registration System, WHO ki modeled estimates. Ek hi crash sab mein dikhna chahiye, phir bhi picture alag-alag hai — kabhi kabhi ek lakh se zyada mauton ka fark. Galat baseline har target ko performance batata hai aur har evaluation ko guess. Sirf paperwork digitize karne se yeh theek nahi hoga. iRAD aur eDAR jaise systems sahi direction mein hain — ek geo-tagged record disconnected forms se better hai. Lekin buri diagnosis bhi digitize ho sakti hai. Agar ek official ko train kiya gaya hai ki failure ki chain investigate karne ki jagah file band kare, toh faster data entry sirf buri judgment ko speed deti hai.

**Sadken jinke baare mein sab jaante hain ki khatarnak hain**

Phir hai sadak khud. Crashfree ka [Project Rakshak](https://crashfreeindia.org/our-work/project-rakshak) — ek youth-led infrastructure effort — us hisse par kaam karta hai jise India routinely ignore karta hai: woh jagahein jo locals pehle se jaante hain ki unsafe hain, lekin formal systems tab action lete hain jab kaafi log hurt ho jaate hain ya mar jaate hain. Ek blackspot ko apna naam mauton se nahi kamana chahiye. Mid-2026 tak, Rakshak teams ne 18 sheher mein 120 se zyada high-risk locations assess ki thi aur 25 par fixes ke approvals mile the — aisi jagahein jahan under-reporting count karein toh shayad 1,000 se zyada crashes ho chuke the, aur expect kiya gaya ki interventions crash risk 20 se 50 percent tak kam karein. Number zyada scale ke liye nahi, method ke liye matter karta hai: khatarnak sadken authorities ko tab dikhaya ja sakta hai, jab aur maut wahan aaye. Kaam mein yeh bhi pata chala ki crash data almost certainly undercount hai, kyunki non-fatal crashes formal memory se gayab ho jaate hain. Sadak ek kahani bata rahi thi. File doosri.

**Crash ke baad bhi opacity maari karti hai**

Yahi break impact ke baad bhi dikhta hai, aur yeh Indian road safety ki sabse kam discuss ki jaane wali failure hai. Ek desh Good Samaritan law pass kar sakta hai — India ka 2016 mein Supreme Court ne diya aur baad mein Motor Vehicles Act ki Section 134A mein likha, jo madad karne wale bystanders ko protect karta hai — ambulance numbers publish kar sakta hai, compensation frameworks announce kar sakta hai. Lekin agar citizens ko apne rights nahi maloom, hospitals unpredictably behave karte hain, aur families ke paas system navigate karne ka koi tarika nahi, toh kaagaz par lika kanoon practice mein koi trust nahi banata. Yeh crash ke pehle minutes mein dikhta hai, jab bheed jama hoti hai aur koi nahi jaanta ki call kise karein ya victim ki help karne ki kya keemat padegi.

Isliye ek first-responder card bhi matter karta hai. Early Crashfree material ne roadside action ko teen shabdon mein dala: Check, Call, Care. Scene safe karo, 112 call karo, bleeding rok, zaroorat ho toh CPR shuru karo. Ek kaam karne wale system mein yeh obvious hota. India mein yeh clarity khud ek missing infrastructure hai. Victim ki madad karna baar baar private himmat aur improvisation nahi mangna chahiye.

Yeh trauma care mein bhi dikhta hai. AIIMS Trauma Centre ki ek visit abstraction ko concrete bana deti hai. Crashfree ke published account ke mutabik, AIIMS roz lagbhag 125 se 175 trauma patients dekhta hai — saal mein 70,000 se zyada — aur kai serious crash victims abhi bhi bina kisi meaningful pre-hospital care ke pahunchte hain. Aam log, aksar rickshaw drivers, bina training aur equipment ke first responders ban jaate hain. Ek leading trauma centre par bhi, pre-arrival chain strikingly thin hai. Isliye PM RAHAT jaisi ek cheez matter karti hai. Uska strongest form compensation-after-the-fact nahi, balki treatment certainty hai impact ke minutes mein: hospitals ready, police aur hospitals synchronized, eligible victims ke liye zero out-of-pocket cost. Ek crash isliye fatal nahi hona chahiye ki chain confused ho ki kaun admit karta hai, kaun pay karta hai, aur kaun pehle move karta hai.

Phir aata hai compensation — jahan law hai lekin access toot hua hai. Supreme Court ne January 2025 mein yahi kaha: ek negligible number ke victims schemes se fayda uthate hain kyunki public awareness low hai aur authorities ki taraf se dissemination kamzor hai. [World Bank ki 2021 ki study](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2021/02/13/road-crashes-in-india-increase-household-poverty-and-debt) mein paya gaya ki crash se hit garib gharano mein se sirf 14% ko koi bhi compensation mila, aur financial blow saat mahine ki household income se zyada tha. Crashfree ka field brief, [Justice Unserved](https://www.crashfreeindia.org/documents/justice-unserved-crashfree-india.pdf), iske andar ki machinery dikhata hai. Hit-and-run claims eligible crashes ka sirf karib 12% per year hain. Survey mein shamil 70% low-income households ko pata hi nahi tha ki compensation exist karta hai. Pending hit-and-run claims mein se 9 mein se 10 paperwork par atke hain, jabki sau crores tribunals mein unclaimed pade hain — sirf Bombay mein ₹459 crore — aur national backlog 10 lakh se zyada cases ka hai, ₹80,000 crore se upar.

Aur usi vacuum mein ghuste hain middlemen. Unregulated "ambulance chasers" victims ko hospitals par intercept karte hain aur commission par claims mein help bechte hain; 2021 mein Bar Council ne 28 advocates suspend kiye fake motor-accident claims file karne ke liye. Jab system opaque hota hai, trust privatize ho jaata hai aur victim ko wapas bechdi jaati hai. India mein mainly compensation-law ka masla nahi hai. Compensation-access ka masla hai.

Fix aksar kisi nayi law se chhota hota hai. Bahut si families ko nahi pata ki claim chhe mahine ke andar file karna hota hai. Sabse bada unlock nikla 90-minute ka awareness session aur ek printed checklist; AIIMS aur Sharda hospital ke andar helpdesks par, 100 se zyada victim families usi ke liye line mein lagi thi. Opacity ka close-up yahi dikhta hai. Logo ko sirf paise nahi chahiye. Unhe translation chahiye. Policy kehti hai victim claim kar sakta hai. Trust ka matlab hai victim ko pata hai, woh believe karta hai, prove kar sakta hai, aur process mein itna survive kar sakta hai ki receive kar sake.

**Chain ka malik koi nahi**

Trust ka masla cultural bhi hai. India abhi bhi sadak maut ko growth ki natural tax maanta hai: zyada gaadiyaan, wider sadken, kuch waqt ke liye badhti fatalities, aur neche ek khamosh shrug. Humne speed, volume, aur aspiration build ki hai. Humne unke barabar ka safety philosophy nahi banaya. Ek line us kaam mein baar baar aayi: information is infrastructure. Kamzor crash data safety design ko kamzor banata hai, invisible hazards prevention ko kamzor banati hain, ek illegible compensation system justice ko kamzor banata hai. Aur iska gehra karan yeh hai ki sab kuch fragmented hai. Road safety India mein transport departments, police, hospitals, local governments, courts, insurers, aur ministries mein bata hua hai. Har koi apna slice dikha sakta hai aur phir bhi sadak par woh insaan ek aisi failure ke andar reh jaata hai jisko end-to-end koi nahi own karta. Isliye India itni sincere road-safety activity produce karta hai aur itna kam visible outcome. Sab ke paas ek fragment hai. Chain ka malik koi nahi.

Ek mobility company jo category ka upside chahti hai, woh downside ko kisi aur ki ministry nahi keh sakti. Isliye yeh ek CSR sidecar ya awareness week nahi ho sakta. Koi bhi company jo scale par mobility se fayda uthati hai, uski kuch zimmedari banti hai ki system ko zyada legible, zyada responsive, ya kam lethal banaye. Safety category ke adjacent nahi hai. Yeh category ke substrate ka hissa hai.

Iska matlab nahi ki Crashfree ne masla crack kar diya. Nahi kiya. Lekin mid-2026 tak usne ek alag model ka outline zaroor dikhaya tha: khatarnak infrastructure ko tab dhundho jab woh aur maut ikhatta kare, compensation ko legal footnote nahi balki justice system mano, sirf reports nahi balki public tools banao, aur research ka use institutions ko move karne ke liye karo, unhe decorate karne ke liye nahi.

Ek mature road-safety system maan leta hai ki insaan galti karenge. Uski zimmedari hai ki woh galtiyon ko kam likely, kam severe, zyada easily respond ho sakne wali, aur zyada easily sikhi ja sakne wali banaye. Iske liye chahiye better design, better information, better coordination, aur zyada trust.

Safer sadken tab shuru nahi hoti jab drivers perfect ho jaate hain. Tab shuru hoti hain jab systems itne trustworthy ban jaate hain ki imperfection itni baar fatal na ho.
