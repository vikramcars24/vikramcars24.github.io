Builder. Ek Wahi Title Jo Mayne Rakhta Hai.

Jab context sasta hota ja raha hai aur coordination compress ho rahi hai, value shift ho rahi hai — un logon se jo sirf kaam ki baatein karte hain, un logon ki taraf jo loop close karte hain.

Seedha Point

  • Puraana prestige system un logon ko reward karta tha jo kaam se door rehte the. Jab context sasta ho, value usi ke paas shift hoti hai jo loop close kare.
  • Cars24 ne VP, director, aur manager ke prefixes hata diye: ab ek hi title hai, Builder — ek sports team jisme captain bhi hota hai, lekin har player apna part khelta hai.
  • Real agency mehenga hota hai. Uska cost uthana hi ownership hai.

Kuch mahine pehle main shaam ko ek refurbishment centre mein ghoom raha tha, jab floor ka zyaadatar kaam khatam ho chuka tha. Ek technician abhi bhi ek car ke neeche lift par tha, ek kaam finish kar raha tha jo agali subah koi aur team easily uthaa sakti thi. Uski shift khatam ho chuki thi. Car ki early delivery scheduled thi. Maine poochha woh abhi bhi kyun hai. Usne kaha ki customer ko car jaldi chahiye thi kyunki ghar mein shaadi thi, aur usne team ko bola tha ki woh ise close karega.

Woh sentence mujhe aksar yaad aata hai. Is liye nahi ki yeh dramatic tha. Is liye ki yeh precise tha. Usne ek open loop dekha aur use close kar diya.

Mujhe lagta hai yahi role hai jo aaj ke modern company mein sabse zyaada mayne rakhti hai. Builder. Builder sirf founders ke liye ek flattering word nahi. Builder sirf engineers ka synonym nahi. Builder woh insaan hai jo dekh sake ki kya hona chahiye aur directly reality badal sake.

Puraana prestige system distance ko reward karta tha

Ek lambe waqt tak companies ek alag tarah ke insaan ko reward karti thi: woh jo kaam se thoda aur door ho, execution karne walon ko coordinate kare, unka status agale layer ki language mein translate kare, aur execution se door rehke legitimacy ikattha kare. Yeh is liye nahi hua ki leaders bure the. Yeh is liye hua kyunki context ikaththa karna mehnga tha aur coordination scarce thi, toh ek scaled company ko coherent rehne ke liye translators, routers, reviewers, aur summarizers chahiye the. Yeh Jay Galbraith ki puraani insight hai — ki organization structure uncertainty mein information process karne ke liye exist karta hai. Humne problem se door rehne ko ek ladder bana diya. Customer ke sabse kareeb baithe insaan ko uss insaan se neeche rakha jaata tha jo customer ko explain karta tha. Code likhne wala uss insaan se neeche tha jo ise ek roadmap update mein convert karta tha — kisi aise insaan ke liye jo ab code jaanna zaroori nahi samajhta tha. Yeh ladder kabhi khoobsurat nahi tha, lekin administratively kaam karta tha. Ab uss usefulness ka ek bada hissa khatam ho raha hai.

Jab context gather karna, move karna, aur act karna sasta ho jaata hai, toh pure coordination ki value girti hai. Zero nahi, lekin itna kaafi hai ki prestige system badal jaaye. Agar systems business ki state visible bana sakti hain, agar messy reality ko teen human relays ke bina summarize kiya ja sake, agar problem ke sabse kareeb wala insaan seedha context tak pahunch sake — toh premium shift ho jaata hai. Highest-value insaan ab automatically woh nahi jो kaam se sabse door ho. Tezi se woh ban raha hai jo problem ke sabse kareeb ho aur actually loop close kar sake. Woh technician jo car finish karta hai kyunki family ko zaroorat hai. Woh inside-sales operator jo customer ko wapas call karta hai kyunki pehla jawab problem solve nahi karta tha. Woh engineer jo struggling system ko fix karta hai outage public hone se pehle. Alag functions, same value: reality mein direct change.

Anti-spectator hai, anti-manager nahi

Is tarah ka koi bhi essay anti-management padhne ka risk le leta hai. Mera woh matlab nahi. Judgment, hiring, standards, coaching, conflict resolution, capital allocation — in sab mein abhi bhi real authority chahiye. Jo kamzor ho raha hai woh management nahi hai. Woh prestige hai jo coordination theater se judi thi: chase emails, status decks, update jо agale layer ke liye reformat ho. Yeh kabhi management ka highest form nahi tha. Yeh overhead tha jise respectable bana diya gaya. Sabse achhe managers bhi builders hote hain. Woh clarity build karte hain, friction remove karte hain, tough calls lete hain, aur apne aaspaas ke logon ki loop-closing capacity badhate hain. Unka contribution yeh nahi ki woh kaam se bach gaye. Woh yeh hai ki woh ise multiply karte hain. Future anti-manager nahi hai. Woh anti-spectator hai.

Isi liye titles companies ke sochne se kam mayne rakhte hain — aur alag tarah se. Ek title ek system mein coordinate hona chahiye, coronation nahi. Use scope, responsibility, aur decision rights batani chahiye. Jis din woh decide karne lage ki kise context milta hai, kise suna jaata hai, aur kaun samajhdaar maana jaata hai chahe problem se proximity ho ya na ho — us din woh useful rehna band ho jaata hai aur gate ban jaata hai. Strong companies ko gates ke liye kam tolerant hona chahiye, is liye nahi ki hierarchy buri hai, balki is liye ki unnecessary distance mehenga hai — speed, accuracy, morale, learning, aur truth ke liye. Customer ko parwah nahi ki escalation path kitna elegant tha. Customer ko parwah hai ki loop close hua ya nahi.

Agency without consequence sirf theater hai

Ek aur wajah hai ki yeh kyun mayne rakhta hai. Jo company kehti hai ki woh builders ko value karti hai, woh yeh pretend karna band nahi kar sakti ki kaam tabhi noble banta hai jab woh consequence se door chala jaata hai. Agar do log dono aise loops close kar rahe hain jo mayne rakhti hain, toh unke beech ka fark domain ka hai, dignity ka nahi. Kai companies us belief ka dawa karti hain aur rozana use tod deti hain — ownership ki theory mein tarif karte hain jabki practice mein status preserve karte hain. Jo log indispensable kaam karte hain woh yeh jhooth bohot pehle feel kar lete hain, is se pehle ki leadership usse maane.

Aur yeh woh hissa hai jo attractive lagta hai jab tak aap ise jee nahi lete. Real agency mehenga hai. Iska matlab hai ki jab call aapka tha aur outcome bura aaya, toh koi chhupne ki jagah nahi hai. Iska matlab hai ki toota hua version bhi aapka hai, aur yeh kehne ki comfort — sahi hote hue bhi bekar — "yeh mera kaam nahi tha," khatam hoti jaati hai. Cost uthana hi ownership banata hai. Baaki delegation hai. Kai log kehte hain ki woh autonomy chahte hain. Kum log consequence chahte hain. Dono alag nahi hain. Isliye Builder ek motivational label nahi hai. Yeh ek structural role hai jiska ek psychological price hai: kam permission, kam ceremony, kam positional status, zyaada exposure, zyaada zimmedaari, aur zyaada aisi dinon ka saamna jahan sawaal aapka level nahi hota — sawaal yeh hota hai ki aapne jo aapka tha woh close kiya ya nahi. Kuch logon ko yeh exhilarating lagta hai. Kuch ko exhausting. Dono reactions honest hain. Market pehle tarah ke insaan ki taraf ja rahi hai.

Aur yahan argument culture se bada ho jaata hai. Uss duniya mein jahan coordination sasta hai, jo insaan loop close kar sakta hai woh uss insaan se tezi se compound karta hai jo sirf route karta hai. Better systems ise accelerate karte hain. AI ise accelerate karta hai. Context tak wider access ise accelerate karta hai. Agale decade ki sabse strong companies dignity, context, aur authority ko baahir ki taraf move karengi — jahan tak problem allow kare — jabki hierarchy ko wahan rakhegi jahan woh quality, safety, ya coherence protect karta hai. Woh yeh pretend karna band kar dengi ki status automatically uss insaan ko milni chahiye jo kaam se sabse door hai. Puraani company ne prestige ko commentary aur execution se distance ke aaspaas banaya. Agali strong company ise problem ke proximity ke aaspaas banayegi.

Cars24 mein humne yeh literally kar diya. Humne puraane bands, grades, aur designations hata diye — vice president, director, manager sab. Ab ek hi title hai, Builder. Aur jab woh prefixes gaye, ek cheez zinda ho gayi: woh insaan jo hamesha capable tha lekin kabhi permitted nahi tha. Jo model hum apne dimag mein rakhte hain woh ek sports team hai. Captain hota hai, lekin har player ka ek part hota hai, aur kaam yeh hai ki apna part khelo — kisi aur ka part supervise mat karo. Kam delegation, zyaada coordination. Yeh main ek reformed offender ki tarah keh raha hoon. Maine yeh company apne haathon se kaam karne ke liye shuru ki thi, aur jaise-jaise hum scale hue, main ek master delegator ban gaya — kaam se aur aur door hota chala gaya, jab tak mere din sirf reviews aur updates mein nahi guzarne lage, na ki building mein. AI ne yeh cheez wapas di. Main ab directly kaam kar sakta hoon, aur wahi sab kar sakte hain — yahi toh poora point hai. Ek company ko ab senior logon ki ek layer ki zaroorat nahi hai jinका kaam sirf review call karna aur troops ko rally karna ho. Woh apne kaam ka part khud karte hain. Sab karte hain.

Yahi mera matlab hai Builder se. Koi heroic identity nahi. Koi flattering label nahi. Ek wahi role jо abhi bhi cleanly compound karta hai jab context sasta hota hai aur reality se chhupna mushkil hota jaata hai.

Notes and Sources

  • Jay Galbraith, "Organization Design: An Information Processing View," 1974. Organization structure uncertainty mein information process karne ke liye exist karta hai; coordination-prestige argument ki neenv.
  • Autonauts, Cars24 ki public culture site (humari apni publication). Is essay mein describe kiye gaye bands, grades, aur designations ka hataya jaana, publicly documented.
