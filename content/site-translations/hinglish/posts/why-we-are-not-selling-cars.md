1960 mein Harvard ke ek professor [Theodore Levitt](https://hbr.org/2004/07/marketing-myopia) ne ek aisa sawaal poocha jisne kai comfortable companies ko hila diya. Aap actually kis business mein ho?

Unka example tha American railroads ka. Woh kabhi giants the, phir decline ho gaye, aur sabne socha transportation ki demand khatam ho gayi. Aisi koi baat nahi thi. Demand badhti rahi. Cars, trucks, aur planes ne us demand ko carry kiya. Railroads ne yeh sab miss kar liya kyunki, Levitt ke words mein, "unhone khud ko railroad business mein samjha, transportation business mein nahi. Woh product-oriented the, customer-oriented nahi." Unhone object ko bachaya aur need ko khod diya.

India ki zyaadatar mobility companies abhi wahi galti kar rahi hain. Woh sochti hain ki woh cars bechne ke business mein hain. Better listing. Better pricing. Better inspection. Faster financing. Zyaada units move karo. Yeh sab matter karta hai, aur yeh sab incomplete hai. Car toh sirf ek visible artifact hai. Product toh trust hai.

Main trust ko brand value ki tarah nahi keh raha. Main use us cheez ki tarah keh raha hoon jo customer actually kharidne ki koshish kar raha hai: asset mein confidence, paperwork mein, financing mein, service mein, transfer mein, recourse mein agar kuch toot jaaye. Confidence ki transaction ke baad bhi reality samajh aayegi. Transaction ek event hai. Ownership ek relationship hai, aur India mein iska ek alag texture hai: woh challan jo mahine baad pata chalta hai, PUC run, woh FASTag jo recharge nahi hota, woh RC jo abhi bhi kisi aur ke naam par hai. Mobility se sentiment hata do toh yahi bacha rehta hai. Log machine nahi khareed rahe. Woh yeh certainty khareed rahe hain ki is machine ka owner hona unhe punish nahi karega.

"Ek car apni design ki wajah se hi ek uncertain machine hai: hazaaron parts aise rate se age ho rahe hain jo bahar se koi dekh nahi sakta, kisi aisi previous owner ke haath mein jo kabhi milne waali nahi. Yahi woh honest reason hai ki log new car ke liye premium dete hain. Woh newness nahi khareed rahe. Woh certainty khareed rahe hain, woh aakela complete certainty jo is category ne kabhi becha hai. Used-car ka poora opportunity yahi hai ki naya car manufacture kiye bina woh certainty manufacture ki jaaye."

**Original tax woh hai jo aap dekh nahi sakte**

Used-car category hamesha ek information problem par chali hai. Buyers ko zyaada pata nahi hota. Sellers prove nahi kar paate. Intermediaries aksar dono se zyaada jaante hain. Documents messy hote hain, history partial hoti hai, asset quality uneven hoti hai. Isliye price sirf price nahi hota. Woh ek wager bhi hota hai us cheez par jo aap dekh nahi sakte.

Yeh wager woh tax hai jo category mein har koi bharta hai, aur isliye inspection, verification, warranty, aur return policies exist karti hain. Lekin woh ek bahut gehri structure ke upar sirf ek layer of comfort hain. Customer actually certainty ka ek stack khareed raha hai jo neeche se upar tak jaata hai: ki vehicle wahi hai jo claim karta hai, ki paperwork aur transfer clean hai, ki financing asset ko honestly price karti hai, ki ownership manageable lagegi adversarial nahi, aur agar kuch galat ho toh system confusion mein nahi ghul jaayega. Ek layer solve karo toh feature milta hai. Poora stack solve karo toh institution milta hai.

Yeh dekha ja sakta hai ki product car nahi hai — car ke alawa sab kuch badlo. Same vehicle rakho, aur transfer clean karo, history legible karo, financing fair karo, aur dekho ki price, resale confidence, aur transact karne ki willingness sab move karti hai. Metal mein kuch nahi badla. Uske around certainty badali.

**Proof un parts mein hai jinका sale se koi lena dena nahi**

Transfer lo. Use administrative residue ki tarah treat kiya jaata hai aur yeh category ka ek purest trust moment hai. Agar previous owner legal exposure mein rehta hai sale ke baad, toh poora market quietly anxious ho jaata hai, aur woh anxiety pricing, resale, aur used khareedne ki social legitimacy mein leak hoti hai. Problem itni real thi ki 2022 mein government ko Motor Vehicle Rules amend karne paday "deemed ownership" create karne ke liye, kyunki registration records real sales se peeche rehte the. Jab state sellers ko un cars se protect karne ke liye ek legal fiction invent kare jo woh ab own nahi karte, toh aap ek trust gap dekh rahe hain, paperwork ki delay nahi. Policy tab product ka hissa ban jaati hai jab public systems ownership ki truth define karte hain.

Ya financing lo. India mein sirf roughly ek-chauthai used cars loan par kharide jaate hain, jabki new ones mein roughly teen-chauthai. Yeh gap mainly capital ka nahi hai. Yeh certainty ka hai. Ek lender kisi aisi asset ko underwrite nahi karega jo use clearly nahi dikhai deti, toh wohi trust rails jo buyer ko calm karti hain — clean history, honest inspection, real title — wahi hain jo credit un logon tak pahunchne deti hain jinhe iska sabse zyaada zaroorat hai. Har clean interaction ek deposit hai, aur woh balance hi hai jo finally asset ko financeable, insurable, aur resaleable banata hai.

Aur woh moment lo jis par category ownership lena pasand nahi karti: jab road dangerous ho jaaye. Ek mobility business jo aapko khareedne mein help kare aur crash hone par, ruke hue compensation claim par, ya kisi investigation mein jab car ke ownership ka thread kho jaaye, gayab ho jaaye — woh poora trust system nahi hai. Ek company jo zyaada logon ko cars mein daalne ka upside uthati hai woh baad mein unke saath jo hota hai uski kuch zimmedari le leti hai. Yeh charity nahi hai. Yeh wohi product hai. Safety aur recourse certainty stack ki last layers hain, aur wahi hain jo customers ko sabse clearly yaad rehti hain jab unhe zaroorat hoti hai.

**Yeh kyun kaehta hai ki Indian auto abhi bhi early hai**

Ek baar aap accept kar lo ki trust hi product hai, toh prize ka size bhi badal jaata hai. India auto demand mein early nahi hai. Used market already roughly ₹4 lakh crore ka hai aur roughly 35 cars per 1,000 logon par, new market se zyaada, saath lakh cars har saal move karta hai. Yeh institutional completion mein early hai. Ownership abhi bhi fragmented hai, information abhi bhi uneven hai, transfer abhi bhi manual hai, aur financing abhi bhi uncertainty ko badly price karti hai. Sirf roughly ek-tehai used-car sales organized players ke through hoti hain. Developed markets mein woh stack already normalized aur invisible hai. Yahan, uske bade hisse abhi bhi ban rahe hain.

Yahi woh opening hai, aur isliye Indian auto aise large companies produce karega jitni logon ko expect nahi hai. Is liye nahi ki cars glamorous hain. Is liye ki ownership unfinished infrastructure hai. Bade outcomes inventory list karne ya units faster move karne se nahi aayenge. Woh itne dense trust rails banana se aayenge ki market differently behave karne lage: kam fear, cleaner transfer, fairer pricing, stronger resale, zyaada durable ownership. Jo company yeh kar le woh kisi purane dealership ki tarah nahi dikhti jiske paas better software ho. Woh platform ki tarah bhi nahi dikhti, kyunki platform buyer aur seller ke beech khadi hoti hai aur toll leta hai. Woh infrastructure ki tarah dikhti hai: woh rails jis par puri category chalti hai, jaise payments rails har transaction ke neeche hoti hain bina khud transaction hue. Aaj build karo toh yeh AI-native vehicle ownership infrastructure hai — rails jo process se utni hi intelligence se bani hain.

Yeh woh thread bhi hai jo is category ke baare mein maine jo likha hai uske neeche chalta hai. Ownership ka aane wala decade tab badhta hai jab fear girta hai, sirf jab supply badhti nahi. Lending affordability ka bridge tab banti hai jab asset par trust ho. Sabse green car woh hai jo pehle se bana hua hai, lekin tabhi agar uski history aur battery par believe kiya ja sake. Road deaths traffic problem se pehle trust problem hain. Yeh alag-alag topics par alag-alag essays nahi hain. Yeh wahi argument hai alag-alag angles se dekha gaya. Product kabhi car nahi tha. Isliye main yeh bhi likhta hoon ki company khud kaise build hoti hai. Aap baaharon certainty nahi bech sakte jabki andar truth slowly move kare.

**Hum kis business mein hain**

Toh jab log poochte hain ki [Cars24](https://www.cars24.com) kya hai, toh honest answer yeh nahi hai ki ek used-car company, usi tarah jaise railroads actually railroad business mein kabhi the hi nahi. Hum cars nahi bech rahe. Car toh artifact hai. Hum ek ki ownership ke fear ko khatam karne ke business mein hain, asset ko legible banana, paperwork ko survivable, financing ko fair, aur system ko itna honest banana ki ek ordinary person ownership mein step kar sake bina yeh soche ki kuch galat hone wala hai.

Levitt ke railroads ke paas customers the, track tha, aur trains thi, aur phir bhi haare, kyunki unhone object ko guard kiya need ki jagah. Indian mobility mein need zyaada cars road par nahi hai. Woh unhe own karne ka confidence hai. Woh build karo, aur cars khud apna khayal rakh lenge.

Hum cars nahi bech rahe. Hum ek khareedne ka confidence bech rahe hain.
