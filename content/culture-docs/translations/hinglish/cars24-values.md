## Problem Ka Malik Ban

Cars24 mein sirf ek role hai — aur woh hai **Builder**. Aaj ke time mein Builder hone ka matlab hai AI-first aur engineering-first sochna. Builders ko koi batane nahi aata ke kya solve karna hai — woh khud dhundhte hain sabse bada problem, aur usse apna lete hain.

Permission ka intezaar nahi karte, kyunki dene wala koi aa nahi raha. Woh bade problems solve karke badte hain, phir usse bhi bade problems, aur phir usse bhi bade.

Builders baar baar fail hote hain — kyunki jo cheez banane layak hai, woh bina fail hue milti nahi. Sirf ek failure count hoti hai — koshish na karna. Hum chahte hain log jo sab ke saamne fail ho sakein, woh dard apne upar le sakein, aur phir dobara uthke try kar sakein. Founder hone ki zaroorat nahi — bas founder ki tarah sochne ka choice karo.

**Test:** Agar jo sabse bada problem tum abhi solve kar rahe ho, uspe thodi si nervous feeling nahi aa rahi — toh woh kaafi bada nahi hai.

---

## Comfort Se Zyada, Sach

Hum woh baat kehte hain jo mushkil hai — room mein, seedha, chahe saamne wale ko buraa lage. Aur jab hum par woh mushkil baat aati hai, hum usi tarah sunte hain — chahe dard ho. Meeting mein haan karna aur baad mein DM pe alag raay rakhna — yeh nahi chalta. Buri khabar seedhi upar jaati hai, sideways nahi.

Hum first principles pe sochte hain — certainties nahi rakhte, hypotheses rakhte hain — chahe woh unpopular kyun na ho. Kyunki comfort wahi jagah hai jahan sach jaake mar jaata hai.

Kabhi kabhi hum apna opinion badal lete hain. Kabhi kabhi hum apni baat pe dadte rehte hain — chahe log hanse, disagree karein, hume khud doubt ho. Sach bolna hamesha galat lagta hai — pehle pehle.

**Test:** Agar koi decision lena hurt nahi kiya, toh shayad woh decision liya hi nahi. Aur agar meeting ke baad tumne kisi ko DM kiya jo tum meeting mein kehna chahte the — toh meeting fail hui, aur tum us failure ka hissa the.

## Aap kya tolerate karte ho, wahi culture ban jaata hai

Low standards ek bimari ki tarah failte hain. Humne slow kaam tolerate kiya hai. Humne sloppy kaam tolerate kiya hai. Humne woh politeness tolerate ki hai jo work ki jagah feelings bachati hai. Iska hamare ko bahut cost hua hai. Jo bhi behaviour hum tolerate karte hain, wahi unwritten policy ban jaati hai. Hum benchmark karte hain best se — sirf better se nahi — kyunki "better" woh trap hai jisme companies atki rehti hain. Attention to detail woh standard hai jo humne sabse zyada slip karne diya. Ab yeh khatam hota hai. Jo hum tolerate karte rahe, uske liye main sabse pehle accountable hoon — aur yahan se bar uthane ke liye bhi main hi sabse pehle accountable hoon.

**Test yeh hai:** Kya aap wahi decision lete agar aapke har trade-off, conversation, aur action sab ko dikhta?

## Ek behtar insaan bano

Jab cheezein sabse mushkil hoti hain, tab hum jo karte hain — wahi hum actually hain. Woh mushkil moments dramatic nahi hote; woh hote hain jab hum quietly kam choose kar sakte the. Ek aisa customer jise pata nahi chalega — uske liye thoda kam care. Jab easy answer milta — thoda kam honesty. Kisi ke jaane ke waqt — thoda kam effort.

Crashfree India, Compassion Fund, aur jis tarah se hum log ko jaane dete hain — yeh sab usi cheez se aaya hai ki humne "less" choose karne se mana kar diya. Hum koi virtuous company nahi banna chahte. Hum chahte hain ki kaam ke zariye hum behtar insaan bante jayein.

**Test yeh hai:** Koi ek recent moment socho jab tum quietly "less" choose kar sakte the. Kya tumne kiya?

## Builder Ki Taraf Se

### Main bhi commit kar raha hoon

Main tumse woh standards nahi maang sakta jo main khud pe nahi lagaata.
Isliye main apne bhi openly bol raha hoon.

Cars24 ka uthna aur girna — dono meri zimmedari hai. Jeetna team ka hota hai. Chookna mera. Jo bhi ho, haalaat chahe kuch bhi hon, mujhe hi rasta nikaalana hai. Jo hum tolerate karte hain woh hamari culture banti hai — aur jo bhi hua, main ne tolerate kiya. Jab do saal pehle humne reset kiya aur utna aage nahi gaye jitna chahiye tha — main bhi utna aage nahi gaya.

Mujhe wohi Builder standard pe measure kiya jayega jo baaki sabko hota hai. Room mein sabse pehle hard baat kehne waala main hounga — aur sabse pehle hard baat sunne waala bhi. Jis din mujhe in values pe hold karna band ho jaaye — uss din mujhe yeh company chalana band kar dena chahiye.
