About

Vikram Chopra

My ambition is to build Cars24 into a $100 billion company from India, with people who think and act like founders.

I co-founded Cars24 because I believed the internet would transform every category, including cars. We began by approaching car owners at a petrol pump and finding buyers through WhatsApp. The idea was simple. Buying or selling something as important as a car should not require you to take a leap of faith.

Today, AI has made the ambition much bigger. I believe it will change what a company can do, how many people it takes, and how quickly it can grow across the world. India built software for the world. I believe we can now build consumer companies for the world, with AI making the judgment and operating capability behind them easier to scale.

I’m learning this by building AI agents myself. It has made me question habits I accumulated over a decade: how we organise work, how decisions get made, and how much time a hard problem should take to solve.

At Cars24, we are rebuilding both the ownership experience and the company behind it. We call our way of working Flatland: no traditional titles or hierarchy, with people organised around problems to solve. I want us to build a company where hundreds of people can think and act like founders, take real ownership, challenge my thinking, and build things I could never have imagined myself.

I keep building because I enjoy it. Learning a new skill, finding a better way, discovering that something I thought was beyond me is now possible. AI has given me more of that feeling than anything since the internet.
